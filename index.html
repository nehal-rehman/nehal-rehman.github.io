About

I am an Embedded Systems / IoT Engineer with hands-on experience in embedded firmware, Embedded Linux, real-time systems, computer vision and edge computing.

My background is in industrial electronics and real-world deployment: microcontrollers, RTOS firmware, industrial communication, computer vision and IoT systems. I am now moving toward research in intelligent autonomous systems, sensor fusion and resilient edge AI.

I am most interested in systems where algorithms cannot be separated from hardware constraints, timing, communication, power, reliability and deployment.

Research direction: embedded systems → edge AI → perception and sensor fusion → state estimation → autonomous systems.

Research interests

  1. Intelligent perception. On-device AI, computer vision, multimodal sensing, IMU and industrial sensors, resource-constrained inference.
  2. Sensor fusion and state estimation. Multi-sensor integration and perception pipelines for robotic and autonomous platforms.
  3. Intelligent edge computing. Edge AI, heterogeneous computing, real-time inference, resilient industrial edge systems.
  4. Autonomous and resilient systems. Autonomous robotics, real-time embedded control, GNSS-resilient navigation.
