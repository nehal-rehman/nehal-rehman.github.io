01 / ABOUT

From engineering to research.

My background is rooted in industrial electronics, embedded firmware and real-world deployment. Over the past several years I have worked across microcontrollers, RTOS-based firmware, Embedded Linux, industrial communication, computer vision, edge computing and IoT systems.

This experience has shaped the research questions I want to pursue: how can sensing, computation and intelligence be brought closer to physical systems so that autonomous platforms can perceive, estimate and act reliably under real-world constraints?

I am particularly interested in systems where algorithms cannot be separated from hardware constraints, timing, communication, power, reliability and deployment.
